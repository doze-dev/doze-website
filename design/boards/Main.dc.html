A real project, not a toy.

Larder is a made-up online grocer: groceries at your door within the hour. It is built the way real backends end up: a monorepo split by domain, each team in the language it knows, held together by Kafka topics and HTTP. Nine services, three languages, five shared backing services, one doze.hcl. No Docker, no compose file, no scripts.

Straight answers

Do I have to give up Docker?

No. Keep containers for building and shipping. doze is for the databases and services you run while you write code.

Is it for production?

No. It is built for your laptop and your CI. That's why it can sleep everything.

What does "asleep" cost me?

The engine's process is stopped and its data stays on disk. One small doze daemon keeps listening and wakes it on the next connection.

Windows?

Not yet. macOS on Apple silicon and Linux today; WSL2 works.

Does it phone home?

No telemetry, no account. It talks to the internet to fetch the engines you declare, and checks their signatures. How we handle security.

What does it cost?

Nothing. Apache-2.0, on GitHub.
