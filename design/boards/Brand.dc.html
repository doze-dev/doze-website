The names

doze is lower case. Always, even first in a sentence.

It is a word, not an acronym, and it is the command you type. The two products beside it keep the name of the thing they stand in for.

doze

The local-development tool: the app, the CLI and doze.hcl. Repo doze-dev/doze.

NotDoze, DOZE, Doze.dev, the Doze CLI
doze AWS

The local AWS emulator. Repo and binary keep the hyphen: doze-aws.

Notdoze-aws in prose, Doze AWS, doze aws, dozeAWS
doze Kafka

The Kafka broker in one Go binary. Repo and binary: doze-kafka.

Notdoze-kafka in prose, Doze Kafka, doze kafka

Hyphens belong to code: repos, binaries, package names. In a sentence, write the name with a space. AWS and Kafka are trademarks of their owners; doze AWS and doze Kafka speak their protocols and are not affiliated with them.

The icons

A sleeping bat on a tile, one colour per product.

Asleep until something needs it: that is the product, and the bat is the whole joke. All three are traced from one bat, so they sit together in a dock or a row of tabs. What it hangs from tells you which one it is.

Using the icon

Use it as it comes. The tile is part of it.

The bat is drawn to read at 16 px on its tile. Take it off the tile, recolour it or squash it and it stops reading as ours.

Do leave a quarter of its width clear on every side.
doze
Do set the name beside it in Bricolage Grotesque 700, lower case, as the site header does.
Do use the same file on light and dark. It carries its own tile.
Don't recolour it. Each colour means a product.
Don't stretch, squash or rotate it. A bat hangs straight down.
DOZE
Don't add outlines, glows or shadows, or set the name in capitals or another face.

Voice

Say the problem first. Then the fix, plainly.

People come to doze because something hurts: fans, ports, a setup doc that worked once. Name that, then say what doze does about it, in the fewest exact words.

1. Problem first
WritePort 5432 is already taken. Every service gets an address of its own, so two projects can both use it.
Notdoze offers a revolutionary, seamless networking experience.
2. Numbers over adjectives
WriteServices go to sleep after 2 minutes with nothing connected.
NotBlazing fast, incredibly lightweight, ultra efficient.
3. Only what is true
WriteNo telemetry, so we can't tell you how many people use it.
NotTrusted by thousands of teams. (Unknown numbers stay in brackets until they are known.)

The sleep words

doze has one metaphor and keeps to it. A service is asleep, dozing or awake; it wakes on a connection and goes to sleep when nothing needs it.
