Port roulette.

Address already in use. Something has 8000. You check: it's Python. Which Python?

And 5432 is a Postgres, but not this project's. It's the one from the client thing last week, still up, holding the port with the wrong data in it.

You keep the ports in your head. Your head is full.

Names, not numbers. And it tells you who's in the way.

Every service gets a name of its own, so two projects can both use 5432 without a clash. When a port is taken, doze says who holds it and offers to move them.

Ask the AI.

Orders can't reach the database. You ask your agent. It's quick, confident, and wrong.

It read every file in the repo. It still can't see your machine: which compose file is up, what's on which port, what you started yesterday and forgot.

It isn't stupid. Nobody told it what's running.

Give it the truth, not a guess.

doze knows what's running, where, and why it stopped. Your agent asks doze instead of guessing from files: doze status --json is the same answer, for machines.
